# Hero architectural drawings

Architect: querkraft architekten, IKEA Vienna Westbahnhof.

- Project: https://www.querkraft.at/en/projects/ikea-city-center
- Published drawings: https://www.querkraft.at/fileadmin/DOCS/publikationen/IKEA-architektur_aktuell-IKEA_wien_westbahnhof-10-2021.pdf
- Source pages: printed pages 106–107 (PDF pages 12–13).
- `architect-plans.js` contains simplified architectural vector paths extracted from the published typical retail plan, hostel plan and rooftop plan. Fine hatching, text, colored figures and landscape image textures are omitted.
- A representative retail plan is reused on five concept levels; a representative hostel plan is used on the sixth. The user requested a six-floor line-building sequence, so this is a six-floor concept adaptation, not six separately verified plans or a measured model of the seven-storey building. Perspective projection and animation are adaptations for the student's concept website.
- Copyright in the original drawings remains with their respective rights holders. This is an unofficial student prototype.

The photograph-based final building asset is the previously generated cutout. It represents the concept rendering, rather than a measured model of the completed building.
